FIND YOUR MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT (MUD)

This page teaches you how to identify the Municipal Utility District for an address and sends you to official tools. It is not a searchable statewide database. (A fuller directory is a future phase.)

HOW TO FIND YOUR MUD

  1. 1Open the TCEQ Water Districts Map Viewer and search the property address (include city and/or ZIP when prompted).
    Official viewer (opens official site)
  2. 2Note the district name and type. Confirm it is a Municipal Utility District (MUD) if that is what you expect. Other district types also appear in the viewer.
  3. 3Cross-check your county appraisal or tax office property record for taxing units / district entities.
  4. 4Match the name to the district website or operator contacts for bills and notices.
  5. 5Buyers: ask title and your agent for the current Notice to Purchaser once you know the district name.

About maps and accuracy: The TCEQ viewer is an official informational tool. GIS products may not be suitable for legal, engineering, or surveying purposes. For closing or dispute questions, rely on title, survey, and district confirmation.
