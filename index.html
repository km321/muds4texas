Texas Municipal Utility Districts (MUDs), explained

A Municipal Utility District (MUD) is a special-purpose local government in Texas that often provides water, sewer, drainage, and related services in growing communities. If you live in one, or you are shopping for a home that sits in one, this site helps you understand what that means in plain language.
