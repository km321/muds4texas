LIVING IN A MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT (MUD)

If you already own a home in a Municipal Utility District, most questions are operational: Which district am I in? Who gets the water payment? What is the tax line on my bill? How do I follow the board? This guide points you to official tools and clear next steps.

WATER AND SEWER BILLS (SEPARATE FROM THE MUD TAX)

Your monthly (or periodic) water/sewer bill is usually handled by the district's operator or billing company, not by "paying the MUD tax again." Common homeowner tasks:

  • Set up online payment with the correct operator portal for your district
  • Update mailing address after refinance or ownership changes
  • Ask about deposits, transfers, and leak adjustments using that operator's published process

Always match the portal to your district name on the bill. For ad valorem MUD tax concepts, use MUD taxes explained.

BOARD MEETINGS AND TRANSPARENCY

Most districts publish meeting information. Practical participation:

  • Read the agenda before you attend
  • Bring parcel-specific questions in writing when possible
  • Use public comment rules for that meeting
  • For records requests, follow the district's posted process

Board members set policy within legal limits. Day-to-day leaks and billing usually go to the operator first.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What MUD district am I in?
Look up the address in the TCEQ Water Districts Map Viewer and confirm against your tax bill entities.
How do I pay my MUD water bill?
Use the operator/billing instructions on your water bill for that district. It is not the same payment as your property tax.
Can I attend meetings?
Public meetings are generally open. Check your district's posted schedule and rules.
