With a MUD vs without

Interactive 3-step comparison for Houston-area new homes. Navigate with step buttons, use Previous/Next, or let it auto-play. Reduced motion shows the final comparison immediately.

Education only. Figures are from published modeled examples and cited research, not a quote for a specific address. Confirm with Notice to Purchaser, tax office, lender, and licensed professionals.

Primary source: ABHR / CBAS modeling. Optional secondary: ~$150k price gap (Meyers / AEI).

Interaction model

  • Step chips: Click any step (1, 2, or 3) to jump directly to that part of the comparison.
  • Previous / Next: Navigate one step at a time using the arrow buttons.
  • Play / Pause: Auto-advance through all steps, or pause to study the current view.
  • Replay: Start the sequence again from Step 1.
  • Keyboard: Left/Right arrows navigate steps. Space toggles Play/Pause when focused.
  • Reduced motion: Jumps directly to Step 3 (full comparison) with no animation.

Mobile behavior

  • Stacked layout: On screens 640px and below, shows one step at a time in a vertical card.
  • Step 1: Without MUD panel with price and income cards.
  • Step 2: With MUD panel with price and income cards.
  • Step 3: Side-by-side comparison table plus "How it's paid" flow.
  • Touch targets: All controls are at least 44px tall for easy tapping.
  • No horizontal scroll: Content fits within viewport at 320px and up.

Story beats

  1. Step 1 - Without MUD: Barren dirt lots behind a locked gate. Infrastructure embedded in price: ~$475k home, ~$3,652/mo, ~$156,500 income to qualify.
  2. Step 2 - With MUD: Ready neighborhood with houses, streets, and utilities. Bond-funded infrastructure: ~$250k home, ~$2,235/mo, ~$95,800 income to qualify.
  3. Step 3 - Compare: Numbers side by side. "How it's paid" flow: Bond → Services → Transparent tax. Reminder that MUD taxes are real and disclosed.
